Actuated Force
==============

An actuated force acts with equal and opposite magnitude on two points, like a linear actuator pushing two parts of a mechanism apart.

.. diagram:: Equal and opposite force arrows on two points, like a linear actuator

Parameters
----------

Units are shown in SI (m, kg, s, N, rad). MOMDYN does not enforce units, so any consistent set works, but angles are always in radians.

.. params::

   name | str | None | — | Name given to the force.
   ref_frame_key | str | 'inertial' | — | Reference frame in which the vector components of the force are defined.
   start_point | str | 'origin' | — | First point upon which the force acts.
   end_point | str | 'origin' | — | Second point upon which the force acts, with negative magnitude.
   force | numeric | 0 | N | Force magnitude.
   x | numeric | 0 | — | X-component of the vector aligned with the force.
   y | numeric | 0 | — | Y-component of the vector aligned with the force.
   z | numeric | 0 | — | Z-component of the vector aligned with the force.

Equations
---------

Force on the start point and on the end point, with magnitude F along the unit vector u:

.. math::

   \mathbf{F}_{\text{start}} = F\,\hat{\mathbf{u}}, \qquad \mathbf{F}_{\text{end}} = -F\,\hat{\mathbf{u}}

Sign conventions & notes
------------------------

* The direction is set by the components x, y and z in ``ref_frame_key``. TODO: confirm that the components are normalized, so that only ``force`` (or ``moment``) sets the magnitude.
* Because the two forces are equal and opposite, an actuated force changes the internal motion of a mechanism but not its net momentum.
