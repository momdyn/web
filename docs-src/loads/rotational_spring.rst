Rotational Spring
=================

A rotational spring-damper connects two frames with a torsion spring that resists their relative angle and a damper that resists their relative angular speed.

.. diagram:: A torsion spring drawn between two frames in the diagram view

Parameters
----------

Units are shown in SI (m, kg, s, N, rad). MOMDYN does not enforce units, so any consistent set works, but angles are always in radians.

.. params::

   ref_point_key | str | 'origin' | — | Key for the point where the rotational spring is visualized.
   first_frame_key | str | 'inertial' | — | Key for the first frame.
   second_frame_key | str | 'inertial' | — | Key for the second frame.
   k | numeric | 0 | N·m/rad | Spring stiffness constant.
   c | numeric | 0 | N·m·s/rad | Damping constant.

Equations
---------

Magnitude of the attractive moment about the axis of relative rotation:

.. math::

   M = k\,\theta + c\,\dot{\theta}

Here θ is the angle between the two frames and θ̇ is their relative angular speed.

.. math::

   \dot{\theta} = \dfrac{d\theta}{dt}

Sign conventions & notes
------------------------

* There is no separate equilibrium angle: the spring is relaxed when the two frames are aligned (θ = 0).
* ``ref_point_key`` only sets where the spring is drawn. It does not change the physics.
