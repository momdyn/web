Rigid Body Box
==============

A rigid body box is a rectangular block of uniform density; its mass and inertia follow from its dimensions.

.. image:: ../img/rigidbodybox.jpg
   :width: 400
   :alt: A rigid body box: a rectangular block of uniform density

Parameters
----------

Units are shown in SI (m, kg, s, N, rad). MOMDYN does not enforce units, so any consistent set works, but angles are always in radians.

.. params::

   name | str | None | — | Key to be used when referencing this box.
   ref_point_key | str | 'origin' | — | Key of the point to which this box is attached, either at its CG, aft, or forward ends.
   ref_frame_key | str | 'inertial' | — | Key of the frame to which this box orientation is defined.
   density | numeric | 0 | kg/m³ | Uniform density of this box.
   length | numeric | 0 | m | Dimension of this box in the X-axis of the body-fixed frame.
   width | numeric | 0 | m | Dimension of this box in the Y-axis of the body-fixed frame.
   height | numeric | 0 | m | Dimension of this box in the Z-axis of the body-fixed frame.
   attach_to | str | 'CG' | — | CG, fore, or aft
   color | str | 'Green' | — | Color of the body in the diagram view (default Green). It does not affect the physics.

Equations
---------

Mass of a box with length ℓ (X), width w (Y), height h (Z) and density ρ:

.. math::

   m = \rho\,\ell\,w\,h

Moments of inertia about the center of gravity:

.. math::

   I_{xx} = \tfrac{m}{12}(w^2 + h^2), \quad I_{yy} = \tfrac{m}{12}(\ell^2 + h^2), \quad I_{zz} = \tfrac{m}{12}(\ell^2 + w^2)

Sign conventions & notes
------------------------

* ``attach_to`` is ``'CG'``, ``'fore'`` or ``'aft'`` and selects which place on the body sits at the point ``ref_point_key``: its center of gravity, or one end of its length.
* The dimensions are measured along the X, Y and Z axes of the body-fixed frame, which is oriented in ``ref_frame_key``.
