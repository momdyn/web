Rigid Body Cone
===============

A rigid body cone is a circular cone of uniform density; its mass and inertia follow from its length and base radius.

.. image:: ../img/rigidbodycone.jpg
   :width: 400
   :alt: A rigid body cone: a circular cone of uniform density along the X axis

Parameters
----------

Units are shown in SI (m, kg, s, N, rad). MOMDYN does not enforce units, so any consistent set works, but angles are always in radians.

.. params::

   name | str | None | — | Key to be used when referencing this cone.
   ref_point_key | str | 'origin' | — | Key of the point to which this cone is attached, either at its CG, aft, or forward ends.
   ref_frame_key | str | 'inertial' | — | Key of the frame to which this cone orientation is defined.
   density | numeric | 0 | kg/m³ | Uniform density of this cone.
   length | numeric | 0 | m | Dimension of this cone in the X-axis of the body-fixed frame.
   radius | numeric | 0 | m | Dimension of this cone in the Y- and Z-axes of the body-fixed frame.
   attach_to | str | 'CG' | — | CG, fore, or aft
   color | str | 'Green' | — | Color of the body in the diagram view (default Green). It does not affect the physics.

Equations
---------

Mass of a cone with length ℓ (X), base radius r and density ρ:

.. math::

   m = \tfrac{1}{3}\,\rho\,\pi r^2 \ell

Moments of inertia about the center of gravity, which is a distance ℓ/4 from the base:

.. math::

   I_{xx} = \tfrac{3}{10} m r^2, \qquad I_{yy} = I_{zz} = \tfrac{3}{80}\,m\left(4 r^2 + \ell^2\right)

Sign conventions & notes
------------------------

* ``attach_to`` is ``'CG'``, ``'fore'`` or ``'aft'`` and selects which place on the body sits at the point ``ref_point_key``: its center of gravity, or one end of its length.
* The axis of the cone is the X axis of the body-fixed frame, which is oriented in ``ref_frame_key``.
