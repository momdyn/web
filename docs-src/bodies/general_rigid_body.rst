General Rigid Body
==================

A general rigid body has a given mass and inertia tensor about its center of gravity, for shapes that the box, cylinder and cone cannot describe.

.. image:: ../img/generalrigidbody.jpg
   :width: 400
   :alt: A general rigid body: a body defined by its mass and inertia tensor about its center of gravity

Parameters
----------

Units are shown in SI (m, kg, s, N, rad). MOMDYN does not enforce units, so any consistent set works, but angles are always in radians.

.. params::

   name | str | None | — | Key to be used when referencing this body.
   ref_point_key | str | 'origin' | — | Key of the point at which this body's CG is located.
   ref_frame_key | str | 'inertial' | — | Key of the frame to which this body orientation is defined.
   mass | numeric | 0 | kg | Mass of this body.
   ixx | numeric | 0 | kg·m² | Moment of inertia (MOI) of this body defined relative to its CG about the reference frame's X-axis.
   iyy | numeric | 0 | kg·m² | MOI of this body defined relative to its CG about the reference frame's Y-axis.
   izz | numeric | 0 | kg·m² | MOI of this body defined relative to its CG about the reference frame's Z-axis.
   ixy | numeric | 0 | kg·m² | Product of inertia (POI) of this body defined relative to its CG in the reference frame's X- and Y-axes.
   iyz | numeric | 0 | kg·m² | POI of this body defined relative to its CG in the reference frame's Y- and Z-axes.
   izx | numeric | 0 | kg·m² | POI of this body defined relative to its CG in the reference frame's Z- and X-axes.
   color | str | 'Green' | — | Color of the body in the diagram view (default Green). It does not affect the physics.

Equations
---------

The inertia tensor about the center of gravity, in the axes of the reference frame:

.. math::

   \mathbf{I} = \begin{bmatrix} I_{xx} & I_{xy} & I_{zx} \\ I_{xy} & I_{yy} & I_{yz} \\ I_{zx} & I_{yz} & I_{zz} \end{bmatrix}

Sign conventions & notes
------------------------

* The center of gravity is at ``ref_point_key``, and the inertia tensor is expressed in the axes of ``ref_frame_key``.
