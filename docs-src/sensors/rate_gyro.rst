Rate Gyro
=========

A rate gyro records how fast a frame is rotating, as an angular velocity in the axes of a chosen frame.

.. diagram:: A rate gyro: the angular velocity vector of a frame, with components in a reference frame

Parameters
----------

Units are shown in SI (m, kg, s, N, rad). MOMDYN does not enforce units, so any consistent set works, but angles are always in radians.

.. params::

   frame_key | str | 'inertial' | — | The frame whose angular velocity is sensed.
   ref_frame_key | str | 'inertial' | — | The frame that the sensed angular velocity is expressed in.

Equations
---------

The sensed quantity is the angular velocity of frame B, resolved in the axes of frame F:

.. math::

   \{\boldsymbol{\omega}_B\}_F = \begin{bmatrix} \omega_x \\ \omega_y \\ \omega_z \end{bmatrix}
