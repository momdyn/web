This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Mobile Multibody Dynamics app for iPhone and iPad ("MOMDYN", "the app") and this website, momdyn.com. It is provided by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("we", "us").

The short version

The app does not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect personal information through it. Your models are stored on your device. The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

What the app does with your data

  • Your models and results stay on your device. Models you create or import are saved in the app's folder on your iPhone or iPad, which also appears as a "MOMDYN" folder in the Files app. All calculations, including the derivation of equations and the simulation, run on your device.
  • You choose what to share. Exports (model files, Python source code, simulation data and plots) go wherever you send them, for example to the Files app or another app. We do not receive them.
  • Settings. Preferences such as the automatic-simulation switch are stored on your device only.
  • No device permissions. The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photo library.
  • No accounts, ads or tracking. The app does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Network use

The app's help section shows pages from our documentation site at momdyn.github.io inside the app. Opening a help page loads it from that site, which is hosted by GitHub Pages. As with any website, GitHub receives standard connection information such as your IP address. See the GitHub privacy statement.
