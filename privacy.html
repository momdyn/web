Privacy Policy for Mobile Multibody Dynamics

Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which the Mobile Multibody Dynamics (MOMDYN) mobile application for iPhone and iPad (the "App") handles user information. It also covers this website, momdyn.dc-engineer.com. This App is owned and operated by DCDC LLC, a sole proprietorship. This policy reflects our commitment to user privacy by minimizing data collection and ensuring that your data remains under your control.

Developer and Contact Information

  • App Owner: DCDC LLC
  • Sole Proprietor/Data Controller: Eliott Radcliffe
  • Contact Email: Eliott.Radcliffe@DC-Engineer.com
  • App Website: momdyn.dc-engineer.com

1. Data Collection and Handling Summary

Models, Calculations and Exports

  • Local processing only: All calculations, including deriving the equations of motion and running the simulation, are performed exclusively on your device.
  • Local storage: Models you create or import are saved in the App's folder on your iPhone or iPad, which also appears as a "MOMDYN" folder in the Files app. Preferences, such as the automatic-simulation switch, are also stored on your device. If you use iCloud sync (described below), your models are also kept in your own private iCloud.
  • No transmission: Your models, results and exports (model files, Python source code, simulation data and plots) are NOT transmitted to DCDC LLC or any third party. With iCloud sync, copies of your models go only to your own private iCloud, which Apple manages. Exports and shared models go only where you choose to send them. We never receive or store this data.
  • No device permissions: The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photo library.
  • No personal identifiers: The App has no accounts of its own. iCloud sync uses your Apple ID, which Apple manages and which we never see. We do not collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers within the App itself.

iCloud Sync (new in version 2.3)

MOMDYN can keep your library of models in sync across your own devices using Apple's iCloud (CloudKit).

  • What is synced: When sync is on, each model you create or open (its name, description, parameters, components, and settings) is stored in your private iCloud database, which is tied to your Apple ID and is accessible only to you.
  • Developer access: DCDC LLC cannot see, read, or access this data. It is not sent to any server operated by us or to any third party, and it is not used for analytics or advertising.
  • Handled by Apple: Sync is handled by Apple and is subject to Apple's own privacy practices.
  • Turning sync off: Sync is on by default for people signed in to iCloud. You can turn it off at any time in the iCloud section of the MOMDYN sidebar. The change takes effect the next time the App opens.
  • Deleting synced copies: Turning sync off stops further syncing but does not remove copies already stored in iCloud. You can delete them in the Settings app, under your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › MOMDYN.
  • Sharing: Models you choose to share, for example as a .mbdz file by AirDrop, Mail, or Messages, are sent only to the recipients you pick.
  • Please note: A model's name and description are text you type, so we recommend not putting sensitive personal information in them.

Analytics and Third-Party Services

  • Analytics: The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services. We only receive aggregated, non-identifiable usage statistics and crash logs provided by Apple (App Store Connect) for users who have agreed to share them, which are not tied to any individual user.
  • Third-party disclosure: Since we do not collect personal data, we have no personal data to sell, rent, or trade with any third party.

Network Use

The App's help section shows pages from our documentation site at momdyn.github.io inside the App. That site is a static page. We receive no information about who opens it, and we see nothing about it through GitHub, which hosts it. As with any website, GitHub, as the host, receives standard connection information such as your IP address; see the GitHub privacy statement. Apart from the help pages and iCloud sync, which communicates with Apple's iCloud servers, the App does not send your data to us or to other servers.

This Website (momdyn.dc-engineer.com)

The sections above describe the App. This website is a static page. It does not use cookies, analytics, feedback forms or third-party fonts or scripts, and we collect nothing from its visitors. A few things to know:

  • Content from Apple: The App Store badge on the home page is an image loaded from Apple's servers (toolbox.marketingtools.apple.com). Your browser requests them directly from Apple, which receives the request your browser makes, including your IP address, browser type and the address of the page you are viewing. We do not control what Apple does with it, so please see Apple's privacy policy.
  • Hosting: The site is a static page hosted by GitHub Pages. We see nothing about visitors through GitHub. GitHub may keep ordinary server logs as described in its privacy statement.
  • Links to other sites: Links to the documentation, YouTube, our other apps and the App Store lead to sites with their own privacy policies.

2. Legal Bases for Processing (For GDPR Compliance)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland, DCDC LLC must have a legal basis to process personal data. Since we do not collect or process any identifiable personal data, this section primarily confirms our approach:

  • Data minimized: The App is designed to require zero collection of personal data by the developer.
  • Legal basis: Any technical data received from Apple (like aggregated usage data) falls under the legal basis of our Legitimate Interests, specifically the need to maintain, update, and improve the App's technical performance and fix bugs.

3. Your Rights as a User (GDPR and CCPA/CPRA)

Even though DCDC LLC does not possess your personal data, we recognize your legal rights regarding any data that might exist. The models you create are stored on your own device, and in your private iCloud if you use sync, under your control.

EU/UK Users (GDPR Rights)

You have the right to request access to any personal data we may hold, to have it corrected, deleted ("Right to Erasure"), or to restrict its processing. Since we do not store your data, exercising these rights involves:

  • Data access/erasure: Your user-generated data is stored on your device and, if iCloud sync is on, in your private iCloud. To delete it, delete the models within the App or in the Files app. Deleting the App removes the data it stores on your device but not copies already in iCloud, which you can delete in the Settings app, under your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › MOMDYN.
  • Lodging a complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority in your country regarding data protection.

California Users (CCPA/CPRA Rights)

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete, and the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Since DCDC LLC does not sell or share any user data and does not collect personal identifiers, these rights are affirmed:

  • Right to know/delete: Your data is stored on your device and, if iCloud sync is on, in your private iCloud, which is controlled by your Apple ID. You control it, and you can delete it as described above.
  • Right to opt out: An opt-out is unnecessary, as we do not sell or share personal data.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children, and we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided us with any personal information, please contact us immediately.
