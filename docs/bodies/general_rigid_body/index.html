General Rigid Body

On this page

On this page

  • Parameters
  • Equations
  • Sign conventions & notes
  • Related

A general rigid body has a given mass and inertia tensor about its center of gravity, for shapes that the box, cylinder and cone cannot describe.

Parameters

Units are shown in SI (m, kg, s, N, rad). MOMDYN does not enforce units, so any consistent set works, but angles are always in radians.

NameTypeDefaultUnitsDescription
namestrNone—Key to be used when referencing this body.
ref_point_keystr'origin'—Key of the point at which this body's CG is located.
ref_frame_keystr'inertial'—Key of the frame to which this body orientation is defined.
massnumeric0kgMass of this body.
ixxnumeric0kg·m²Moment of inertia (MOI) of this body defined relative to its CG about the reference frame's X-axis.
iyynumeric0kg·m²MOI of this body defined relative to its CG about the reference frame's Y-axis.
izznumeric0kg·m²MOI of this body defined relative to its CG about the reference frame's Z-axis.
ixynumeric0kg·m²Product of inertia (POI) of this body defined relative to its CG in the reference frame's X- and Y-axes.
iyznumeric0kg·m²POI of this body defined relative to its CG in the reference frame's Y- and Z-axes.
izxnumeric0kg·m²POI of this body defined relative to its CG in the reference frame's Z- and X-axes.
colorstr'Green'—Color of the body in the diagram view (default Green). It does not affect the physics.

Equations

The inertia tensor about the center of gravity, in the axes of the reference frame:

Sign conventions & notes

  • The center of gravity is at ref_point_key, and the inertia tensor is expressed in the axes of ref_frame_key.
