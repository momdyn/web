Translational Spring

On this page

On this page

  • Parameters
  • Equations
  • Sign conventions & notes
  • Related

A translational spring-damper connects two points with a spring that resists stretching and a damper that resists their relative speed.

Parameters

Units are shown in SI (m, kg, s, N, rad). MOMDYN does not enforce units, so any consistent set works, but angles are always in radians.

NameTypeDefaultUnitsDescription
start_pointstr'origin'—Key for the first point.
end_pointstr'origin'—Key for the second point.
knumeric0N/mSpring stiffness constant.
cnumeric0N·s/mDamping constant.
equilibriumnumeric0mEquilibrium length of the spring.

Equations

Magnitude of the attractive force along the line from the start point to the end point:

F=k (x−e)+c x˙F = k\,(x - e) + c\,\dot{x}F=k(x−e)+cx˙

Here x is the distance between the two points, e is the equilibrium length and ẋ is the rate at which that distance changes.

x˙=ddt ∥rend−rstart∥\dot{x} = \dfrac{d}{dt}\,\lVert \mathbf{r}_{\text{end}} - \mathbf{r}_{\text{start}} \rVertx˙=dtd​∥rend​−rstart​∥

Sign conventions & notes

  • A positive F pulls the points together, so the spring is relaxed at the equilibrium length e and pulls the points together when stretched beyond it.

  • MOMDYN creates parameters for the stiffness, damping and equilibrium, named from the two points (for points O and a: k_O/a, c_O/a and e_O/a).
